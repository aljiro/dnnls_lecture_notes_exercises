## Reveal: what was this experiment really about?

The model learned, in its parameters, **a procedure for using demonstrations**. The specific key-to-label mapping was not stored permanently because that mapping changed from episode to episode.

At inference time, the weights stayed fixed. Changing the demonstrations changed the model's behaviour because temporary task information was present in the context. This is the central operational idea behind **in-context learning**: behaviour adapts from examples or instructions supplied to the forward pass without a gradient update to the model parameters.

That does not mean every prompt-conditioned response should automatically be called learning. The useful distinction is to ask **what changed**: the weights, the input context, an external memory, or only the amount of computation.
