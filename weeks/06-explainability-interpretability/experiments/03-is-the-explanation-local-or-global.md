## Reveal: what was this experiment really about?

Different explanation tools answer **different questions**.

Permutation importance asks what happens to performance across a dataset when one feature's relationship to the target is broken. A local surrogate asks which simple linear approximation best mimics the model near one particular input. A counterfactual asks what change would alter one particular decision.

Those answers need not agree, especially with nonlinear interactions and correlated features. Disagreement is not automatically a bug; it may reflect a change in the question being asked.
