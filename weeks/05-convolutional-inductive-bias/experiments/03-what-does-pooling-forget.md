## Reveal: what was this experiment really about?

Pooling is not merely a standard CNN ingredient. It makes an explicit trade-off.

Spatial aggregation can make a representation more **invariant** to where a feature occurs, which is useful when the task only cares whether that feature is present. But invariance is obtained by making some previously different inputs look the same. Once location information has been removed, a downstream classifier cannot recover it by training longer.

The right amount of spatial compression therefore depends on the question being asked. A representation useful for classification may be too coarse for localisation, detection or reconstruction.
