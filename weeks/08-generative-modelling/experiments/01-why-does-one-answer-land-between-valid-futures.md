## Reveal: what was this experiment really about?

The experiment separated **point estimation** from **distribution modelling**.

When several futures are valid, an MSE model is rewarded for predicting a conditional mean and an L1 model a conditional median. Those summaries can lie between actual modes. A stochastic model has a different job: represent enough of the conditional distribution that multiple plausible samples can be drawn.

This is the same structural problem that made the Week-4 StoryReasoning pixel median competitive: the next observation is not uniquely determined by the context.
