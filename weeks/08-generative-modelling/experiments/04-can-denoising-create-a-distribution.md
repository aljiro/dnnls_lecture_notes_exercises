## Reveal: what was this experiment really about?

Diffusion turns generation into a sequence of **small denoising decisions**. Instead of asking one network to jump directly from random noise to a data point, the model learns how noise behaves at many corruption levels and repeatedly moves samples back toward the data distribution.

The experiment also reinforces that generative quality is multidimensional: samples should be close to the data **and** cover the modes rather than collapsing onto only a few.
